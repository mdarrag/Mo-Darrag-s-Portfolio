How I work

Four habits that survive contact with reality

Everything below shows up in the case studies, not just on this page.

Size the prize before picking the fight

The worst conversion rate is rarely the best opportunity. I pick the fight on money at stake and on whether I can name the cause — a category with a third of the revenue and a fixable gap beats one with a terrible rate and one booking behind it.

Write the hypothesis so it can be wrong

Every bet gets a falsification clause and a decision rule agreed before launch: if this signal moves and the metric doesn't, I read a correlation as a mechanism — and I say so rather than re-cutting the data until it agrees with me.

Guardrails decide the ship

A primary metric can be bought with refunds. So cancellations, supplier impressions and absolute counts — not just rates — sit beside the KPI, and any breach blocks the release rather than getting monitored into oblivion.

Set clear goals, then step back

I've led PMs through mergers, expansions and rebuilds. What works is ownership with real autonomy, career paths co-created rather than handed down, and feedback given often enough to be unremarkable.
